The 44/60 Shift Checklist

Navigating the Biomolecular Aging Bursts. Based on the 2024 Stanford study revealing non-linear aging shifts at 44 and 60.

Phase 1: The 44-Year Shift (Lipid & Alcohol Metabolism)

Target: Cardiovascular health, skin elasticity, and metabolic flexibility.

🛡️ Lipid Defense Protocol

  • Advanced Panel Screening: Test for ApoB and Lp(a), not just LDL-C.
  • Omega-3 Index: Aim for >8% (erythrocyte levels).
  • Fiber Intake: Increase soluble fiber to 30g+ daily.

🍷 Alcohol & Liver Resilience

  • Audit Consumption: Reduce alcohol intake; metabolic ability drops here.
  • Liver Support: Consider NAC or Milk Thistle if consuming.

🧬 Skin & Matrix Protection

  • Collagen Support: 10-15g hydrolyzed collagen peptides daily.
  • UV Defense: Daily broad-spectrum SPF as barrier function declines.

Phase 2: The 60-Year Shift (Immune & Kidney Function)

Target: Inflammaging, immune senescence, and renal preservation.

🦠 Immune Fortification

  • Protein Pulse: Increase protein to 1.6g/kg to combat sarcopenia.
  • Zinc & D3 Status: Maintain optimal serum levels.
  • Senolytic Review: Discuss protocols with your physician.

🩸 Kidney & Glucose Stability

  • Cystatin C Test: More accurate than Creatinine for kidney function.
  • Glucose Disposal: Post-meal walks (10-15 mins).
  • Hydration Strategy: Consistent fluid intake.
