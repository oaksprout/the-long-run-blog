# Research Report: Urolithin A & Immune Rejuvenation

## Overview
A landmark randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial published in *Nature Aging* (January 2026) has demonstrated the significant potential of Urolithin A (UA) to rejuvenate the human immune system. The study focused on healthy middle-aged adults and investigated how UA, a mitophagy inducer, impacts age-related immune decline.

## Key Scientific Findings
The study revealed that Urolithin A supplementation acts as a powerful metabolic reset for immune cells:

1.  **Rejuvenation of Naïve CD8+ T Cells:**
    *   Significant increase in the pool of **naïve CD8+ T cells**, which are essential for recognizing and responding to new viral threats and cancers.
    *   A marked reduction in markers of T-cell **exhaustion**, effectively shifting the immune profile toward a more "youthful" state.
2.  **Mitochondrial Biogenesis and Metabolic Shift:**
    *   The intervention stimulated the creation of new, healthy mitochondria (mitochondrial biogenesis) within immune cells.
    *   Immune cells shifted their primary fuel source from glucose to more efficient substrates like **fatty acids and amino acids**, reducing "inflammaging" markers.
3.  **Enhanced Immune Fitness:**
    *   Participants showed improved overall immune resilience and a faster response to cellular stress.
