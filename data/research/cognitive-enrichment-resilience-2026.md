# Research Artifact: Lifetime Cognitive Enrichment and Resilience (2026)

## Study Identification
- **Title:** Associations of Lifetime Cognitive Enrichment With Incident Alzheimer Disease Dementia, Cognitive Aging, and Cognitive Resilience
- **Journal:** *Neurology* (February 11, 2026)
- **Authors:** Andrea R. Zammit, et al.
- **Relevance:** Provides quantitative evidence for the "Brain Dividend" — a 5-year delay in the onset of Alzheimer's dementia through lifelong cognitive activity.

## Key Findings
- **Risk Reduction:** One unit higher in lifetime enrichment was associated with a 38% lower hazard of developing AD dementia.
- **Onset Delay:** High lifetime enrichment (90th percentile) resulted in a mean 5-year delay in dementia onset compared to low enrichment (10th percentile).
- **MCI Delay:** For mild cognitive impairment, the delay was 7 years.
- **Cognitive Reserve:** Higher enrichment maintains memory and thinking skills even in the presence of physical brain pathology.
