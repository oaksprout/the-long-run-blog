# MIR Light Longevity Research (2026)

## 1. Mid-infrared light produces anti-ageing effects through resonant absorption by living organisms
- **Source:** Shao, C. et al. *bioRxiv* (February 10, 2026).
- **Key Finding:** Exposure to super-weak 34-THz mid-infrared (MIR) light (approximately 1 μW·mm⁻²) significantly prolonged the median lifespan of *Caenorhabditis elegans* by 60%.
- **Mechanism:** Resonant absorption by phosphate groups (PO₄) in nucleic acids and mitochondrial phospholipids (33–35 THz range).
- **Efficiency-First Model:** Unlike traditional antioxidants or caloric restriction mimetics, MIR light resonantly enhances the kinetic efficiency of integrated cellular operations without activating stress-response pathways.
- **Cellular Impact:** Enhanced global gene transcription and promoted a mitochondrial state of high-efficiency energy metabolism.
- **Protocol Application:** Potential for non-biochemical, physical modulation of aging via frequency-specific light therapy.
