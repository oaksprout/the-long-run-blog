# Research Report: Dr. Peter Attia & The Centenarian Career (Jan 2026)

## Overview
This report synthesizes the latest (2025-2026) research, public stances, and philosophical frameworks of Dr. Peter Attia, specifically focusing on how his "Medicine 3.0" and "Centenarian Decathlon" models can be applied to professional longevity and cognitive resilience.

## Key Themes for 2025-2026

### 1. The Centenarian Decathlon for Knowledge Workers
Attia's core framework involves identifying the physical tasks you want to do at age 100 and reverse-engineering the training.
*   **Key Insight:** For the 100-year life, we must also identify "Cognitive & Professional Tasks" for age 80-90.
*   **Application:** If you want to consult or write at 90, you need to train for "Cognitive Stability" and "Executive Power" now.

### 2. GLP-1 Agonists: Beyond Weight Loss (The Brainspan Angle)
In late 2025, Attia highlighted the cardiovascular and neuroprotective benefits of GLP-1s that appear independent of weight loss.
*   **Stance:** They are "foundational medicines" for longevity, but they "up the ante" for strength training.
*   **Protocol:** GLP-1 + High Protein + Heavy Resistance Training = The 2026 Gold Standard for metabolic and cognitive health in high-stress careers.

### 3. Stability as the Foundation of Career Longevity
Attia emphasizes stability (foot health, core, balance) as the "floor" of physical health.
*   **Professional Parallel:** "Occupational Stability" – the emotional and psychological balance needed to handle the volatility of a 60-year career without burnout-induced "injury."

### 4. Zone 2 and the "Executive Engine"
Attia remains adamant about 3-4 hours of Zone 2 training.
*   **Cognitive Benefit:** Enhanced mitochondrial efficiency in the prefrontal cortex leads to better decision-making under pressure.
