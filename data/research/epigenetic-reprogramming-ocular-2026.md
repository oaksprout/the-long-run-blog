# Epigenetic Reprogramming for Ocular Aging (Sinclair Lab 2026)

## Research Overview
- **Source:** Lee, J., et al. "Epigenetic Reprogramming for Ocular Aging and Disease: Mechanisms, Biomarkers, and the Road to the Clinic." *Progress in Retinal and Eye Research* (January 21, 2026).
- **Key Finding:** Selective expression of Yamanaka factors (OSK) can reset the DNA methylation age of retinal ganglion cells, restoring vision in models of glaucoma and age-related decline.
- **Context:** This paper provides the scientific foundation for the FDA-cleared ER100 clinical trials initiated in early 2026.

## Protocol Translation
- **Core Components:** AAV2-mediated delivery of *Oct4*, *Sox2*, and *Klf4* (OSK factors).
- **Mechanism:** Fully inducible system where OSK expression is controlled by Doxycycline (Dox) administration.
- **Safety Protocol:** Deliberate exclusion of the *c-Myc* oncogene to allow for cellular rejuvenation without dedifferentiation or tumor formation.
- **Application Strategy:** 
    1. Subretinal or intravitreal injection of the AAV-OSK vector.
    2. Post-injection stabilization period (7-10 days).
    3. "Rejuvenation Window": Doxycycline-induced OSK expression for 3-4 weeks.
    4. Monitoring via Horvath-style epigenetic clocks to confirm biological age reduction.
- **Target Metrics:** Restoration of axonal transport, mitochondrial efficiency, and visual acuity.
