# Content Brief: Organ Aging Quiz Logic

**Title:** Which Organ is Leading Your Aging Race?
**Concept:** A diagnostic-style quiz to help users identify their potential "Weakest Link" organ system based on the 2025 Nature Medicine study findings.

## Logic Structure

### Category 1: Brain (The CEO)
*   **Question:** Do you experience "brain fog" or word-finding difficulties after 3 PM?
*   **Question:** How is your sleep latency (time to fall asleep)? (>20 mins indicates potential neural sympathetic overdrive).
*   **Signal:** High frequency of cognitive fatigue = Brain Acceleration Risk.

### Category 2: Heart/Vascular (The Engine)
*   **Question:** What is your recovery time after a flight of stairs?
*   **Question:** Do you have cold hands/feet regularly?
*   **Signal:** Poor perfusion/recovery = Vascular Acceleration Risk.

### Category 3: Metabolic (Liver/Pancreas - The Factory)
*   **Question:** Do you get "hangry" or shaky if you miss a meal?
*   **Question:** Where do you store fat? (Visceral/Midsection = Metabolic risk).
*   **Signal:** Glucose instability = Metabolic Acceleration Risk.

### Category 4: Kidney (The Filter)
*   **Question:** Is your hydration constant but you still feel thirsty?
*   **Question:** Do you have puffiness under the eyes in the morning?
*   **Signal:** Fluid regulation issues = Kidney Acceleration Risk.
