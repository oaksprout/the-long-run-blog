# Research Report: Human Longevity Milestones (2025-2026 Review)

## Executive Summary
The years 2025 and 2026 have marked a pivotal shift in longevity science from animal models to large-scale human clinical outcomes. Key interventions like Rapamycin and Urolithin A have moved beyond "potential" to demonstrated efficacy in healthspan, muscle preservation, and neuroprotection.

## 1. The PEARL Trial: Rapamycin’s First Major Human Signal
The *Participatory Evaluation of Aging with Rapamycin for Longevity* (PEARL) trial, the first large-scale, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of Rapamycin in healthy humans, released its landmark results in April 2025.

### Key Outcomes
*   **Safety Profile:** Confirmed that low-dose, intermittent Rapamycin (5mg-10mg weekly) is safe and well-tolerated in adults aged 50-85.
*   **Muscle Preservation (The "Women’s Signal"):** 
    *   Women taking 10mg weekly experienced a **5% increase in lean tissue mass** over 48 weeks compared to baseline.
    *   This is a significant discovery, as Rapamycin (an mTOR inhibitor) was previously feared to potentially cause muscle wasting.
*   **Visceral Fat:** While the primary endpoint of visceral fat reduction across all groups was not statistically significant, the study observed that men in the placebo group gained visceral fat while those on Rapamycin remained stable.
*   **Quality of Life:** Significant improvements in self-reported pain and emotional well-being were noted in the treatment groups.

### Critical Insight: The Bioavailability Bottleneck
The PEARL trial highlighted that **compounded Rapamycin** often has poor bioavailability (low absorption). This finding has led to a 2026 shift toward enteric-coated or pharmaceutical-grade formulations to ensure therapeutic blood levels.

## 2. Neuroprotection & The REACH Trial (March 2026)
As of early 2026, the focus has shifted to the brain. The **REACH trial** (*Rapamycin for Early Alzheimer’s and Cognitive Health*) is expected to report its results in March 2026.
*   **Objective:** To determine if pulsed Rapamycin can slow cognitive decline in patients with early-stage Alzheimer’s or mild cognitive impairment.
*   **Mechanism:** Reducing neuroinflammation and clearing toxic protein aggregates (tau/amyloid) via upregulating autophagy.

## 3. Urolithin A: The Mitophagy & Brain Health Frontier
Urolithin A (UA) has emerged as the premier compound for mitochondrial quality control (mitophagy) in 2026.

### The 650-Participant Brain Study (NCT07060898)
Timeline Nutrition launched its largest human trial to date in late 2025, with 650 participants. 
*   **Focus:** Cognitive performance, mental energy, and sleep quality.
*   **Significance:** This trial moves UA from a "muscle supplement" to a "neuro-longevity" essential.

### Immune Rejuvenation (Nature Aging, Jan 2026)
A landmark study confirmed that 500mg of UA daily reverses **"inflammaging"** by stimulating mitophagy in T-cells. By clearing out "stale" mitochondria, UA restores the metabolic flexibility of the immune system, effectively "younging" the immune response.

## 4. Synergy & Future Dimensions: Pulsed Dosing
The 2026 protocol consensus is moving toward **"Pulsed Longevity"**:
*   **Rapamycin:** Once-weekly dosing to trigger autophagy without chronic mTORC2 inhibition.
*   **Urolithin A:** Daily dosing to maintain a "basal rate" of mitophagy.
*   **The Synergy:** Combining UA’s mitochondrial cleanup with Rapamycin’s broader cellular protein cleanup (autophagy) provides a two-pronged defense against cellular senescence.
