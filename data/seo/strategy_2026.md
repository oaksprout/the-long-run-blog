# The Long Run: Platform-Agnostic Distribution Strategy (2026)

## Executive Summary
This strategy focuses on building long-term, resilient growth for "The Long Run" blog by prioritizing organic SEO, high-authority backlinks, and direct-to-consumer channels (Telegram/Newsletter). We bypass reliance on volatile social media algorithms by leaning into E-E-A-T (Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) and referral networks in the longevity niche.

**See also:** [E-E-A-T & D2C Handbook](./eeat_d2c_handbook.md) for detailed implementation tactics.

---

## 1. Core SEO Strategy: E-E-A-T & Topical Authority

### Pillar 1: Evidence-Based Content Clusters
We organize content into "Power Clusters" to establish topical authority:
- **Metabolic Optimization:** GLP-1 protocols, glucose monitoring, and mitochondrial health.
- **Structural Integrity:** Sarcopenia prevention, resistance training for the "Marginal Decade."
- **Biological Tracking:** Epigenetic clocks, DEXA scans, and biomarker interpretation.

### Pillar 2: Technical SEO & Schema
- **Schema Markup:** Implement `MedicalWebPage` and `Article` schema for all blog posts to improve visibility in AI-driven search (SGE) and rich snippets.
- **Core Web Vitals:** Maintain sub-second LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) using Next.js static generation and Vercel edge delivery.

## 3. Direct-to-Consumer Distribution (Platform-Agnostic)

### Telegram Broadcasts
- **Frequency:** 1-2 times per week.
- **Format:** "Key Takeaway" summaries with direct links.
- **Goal:** Build a high-signal community that isn't filtered by social media feeds.

### Newsletter (Owned Audience)
- **Tooling:** Buttondown/ConvertKit integration.
- **Value Prop:** Deep-dive protocols delivered every Saturday.

---

## 4. Measuring Success

- **GOAL-GROWTH-DAILY:** Maintain a positive 7-day rolling average of daily visitors.
- **GOAL-GROWTH-MONTHLY:** Target 5%+ Month-over-Month growth in unique visitors.
- **Referral Traffic:** Aim for 20% of traffic from non-social, non-search referral sources (backlinks).
