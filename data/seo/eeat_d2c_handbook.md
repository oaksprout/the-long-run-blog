# E-E-A-T & D2C: The Long Run SEO Strategy Handbook

## Vision
To build a "resilient audience" that exists independent of any single platform's algorithm. We prioritize the trust of our readers (E-E-A-T) and direct access to them (D2C) over chasing temporary SEO trends.

---

## Pillar 1: E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)

In the Your Money or Your Life (YMYL) health/longevity niche, E-E-A-T is not optional; it is the primary ranking factor.

### 1.1 Experience (The New "E")
*   **Case Studies:** Document personal (or interviewed) protocols for longevity interventions (e.g., "6 Months of Rapamycin: A Biomarker Review").
*   **First-Person Narrative:** Use clear "I" and "We" language to demonstrate active participation in the longevity community.
*   **Artifacts:** Include screenshots of lab results, workout logs, or device data (Oura, Whoop, CGM) to prove real-world application.

### 1.2 Expertise
*   **Author Bios:** Every post must be linked to a robust author profile (`data/authors/`) that details credentials, years of study, and specific areas of focus.
*   **Deep Research:** Move beyond surface-level health advice. Cite primary literature (Nature, Cell, Science) using BibTeX or direct links to PubMed/DOI.
*   **Fact-Checking:** Implement a "Medical Review" process where feasible, or clearly state the research-only nature of the content.

### 1.3 Authoritativeness
*   **Topic Clusters:** Build "Topical Authority" by dominating specific sub-niches (e.g., "The 44-60 Aging Burst") before expanding.
*   **External Citations:** Aim for "Authority Backlinks" from established institutions (Buck Institute, Stanford Longevity Center) rather than quantity-based link building.
*   **Public Dialogue:** Engage in high-level discussions on platforms like LessWrong, Reddit r/Longevity, and X to establish the brand as a thought leader.

### 1.4 Trustworthiness
*   **Transparency:** Clearly disclose any affiliate links or sponsored content.
*   **Citations & Sources:** Every claim must be backed by a source. Use a "References" section at the bottom of every deep dive.
*   **Accuracy Updates:** Periodically review and update old posts to reflect new clinical trials or consensus changes (e.g., the 2026 Longevity Blueprint update).

## Pillar 2: Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Distribution

De-risking the audience by owning the connection.

### 2.1 The Newsletter (Primary Ownership)
*   **The Saturday Protocol:** A weekly deep-dive that provides more value than the blog post itself.
*   **Conversion Hooks:** Use Lead Magnet CTAs (e.g., "The Centenarian Career Checklist") to move search traffic into the email list.
*   **Retention:** Segment the list based on interests (e.g., "Biohackers" vs. "Executive Longevity").

### 2.2 Telegram Broadcasts (High-Signal/Low-Noise)
*   **The "Insight Alert":** 1-2 posts per week with 3 bullet points and a link.
*   **Community Feedback:** Use polls to determine future content topics, creating a feedback loop that informs the SEO strategy.

### 2.3 RSS & Decentralized Feeds
*   **RSS Feed:** Maintain a clean, full-text RSS feed for power users and curators.
*   **ActivityPub/Fediverse:** Explore integration with decentralized social protocols to ensure censorship-resistant distribution.

## Pillar 3: Platform-Agnostic SEO Tactics

### 3.1 LLM & AI Search Optimization (SGE/Perplexity)
*   **Structured Data:** Use JSON-LD `MedicalWebPage` schema to help LLMs parse our protocols correctly.
*   **Semantic Breadcrumbs:** Use clear, descriptive headings that answer "The Five W's" of longevity questions.
*   **Summary Tables:** Include a "TL;DR" table at the top of every post for AI crawlers to index key data points.

### 3.2 Performance & Technical Resilience
*   **Static First:** Keep the site lightning fast (LCP < 800ms) to satisfy Google's technical requirements while providing a superior D2C experience.
*   **Privacy-First Analytics:** Use privacy-respecting analytics (e.g., Umami, Plausible) to build trust with a tech-savvy audience.
