# Welcome to The Long Run

We're on a mission to help you live longer, healthier lives by translating the latest longevity research into practical, actionable insights.

## What We Cover

**Protocol Translation**: We take dense research papers from journals like Nature Aging, Cell, and bioRxiv and break them down into clear, implementable lifestyle protocols.

**Myth Busting**: The health and wellness space is full of trends. We use rigorous scientific analysis to separate what works from what doesn't.

**The 100-Year Life**: Beyond the biology, we explore the philosophy and practicalities of living a significantly longer life.

## Our Approach

We're not doctors - we're researchers who explain complex biology in plain English. We're optimistic but rigorous, avoiding sensationalism while prioritizing clarity and actionable insight.

Stay tuned for deep dives into the science of aging, evidence-based protocols, and everything you need to optimize your healthspan.
