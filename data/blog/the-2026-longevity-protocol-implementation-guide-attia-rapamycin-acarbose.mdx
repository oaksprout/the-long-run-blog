# The 2026 Longevity Protocol: A Practical Implementation Guide

Longevity research has reached a tipping point. In 2026, we are no longer guessing which interventions might work; we are layering proven synergies into a cohesive framework. 

This guide translates two of the most significant pillars of current longevity science—**Dr. Peter Attia’s Medicine 3.0** and the **NIA ITP Rapamycin-Acarbose Synergy**—into a practical, actionable protocol for the "Century Life Architect."

---

## Pillar 1: The Attia Framework (The Foundation)

Dr. Peter Attia’s "Medicine 3.0" isn't about treating disease; it's about **delaying its onset** by decades. To implement this, you must shift your focus from "health" to "stability and reserve."

### 1. The Centenarian Decathlon
Identify the 10 physical tasks you want to perform at age 100. 
*   **Action:** If you want to carry your own groceries or pick up a great-grandchild (30 lbs) at 100, you need to be able to deadlift 100+ lbs in your 50s and 60s to account for the natural rate of decline.
*   **Protocol:** 3 sessions of heavy resistance training per week, focusing on compound movements (squat, hinge, push, pull).

### 2. Zone 2: The Metabolic Flush
Zone 2 training (steady-state aerobic exercise where you can still hold a conversation) optimizes mitochondrial efficiency.
*   **Action:** Aim for 180–240 minutes per week. 
*   **Why:** Enhanced mitochondrial health in the prefrontal cortex is a primary driver of cognitive resilience in high-stress professional roles.

## Pillar 2: The Synergy Protocol (The Accelerator)

The National Institute on Aging's Interventions Testing Program (ITP) 2025 review confirmed that the combination of **Rapamycin** and **Acarbose** is the most potent pharmacological lifespan extender discovered to date in mammals.

### 1. The Rapamycin/Acarbose Duo
The synergy is simple: Rapamycin inhibits mTOR (cellular repair), while Acarbose blunts glucose spikes (metabolic stability).

*   **The Findings:** Up to a **37% increase** in median lifespan for males and **28%** for females.
*   **The Synergy:** Acarbose acts as a "metabolic buffer," preventing the slight glucose elevations that can sometimes occur with chronic Rapamycin use.

### 2. Practical Implementation (Medicine 3.0 Approach)
*   **Step A: Baseline Bloodwork.** Check HbA1c, Fasting Insulin, and ApoB. You cannot manage what you do not measure.
*   **Step B: The Acarbose Buffer.** For those consuming carbohydrates, 25-50mg of Acarbose with the first bite of a "high-spike" meal can keep glucose stable.
*   **Step C: The Rapamycin Pulse.** Intermittent dosing (e.g., 5-6mg once weekly) is the current 2026 standard for modulating mTOR without compromising the immune system.
