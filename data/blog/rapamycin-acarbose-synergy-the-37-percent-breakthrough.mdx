# Rapamycin & Acarbose: The 37% Synergy Breakthrough

Do you follow aging science? Then you know **Rapamycin**. It is a top drug in the NIA ITP. It helps mice live longer in labs. 

But things are changing in 2026. We are not just looking at one drug. We want to see how drugs work together. This is called **synergy**. 

Combining Rapamycin and **Acarbose** is now the best way to help health. New data shows life gains of nearly **37%**. This is a huge shift.

---

## 1. How They Work Together

To understand the shift, look at each drug.

- **Rapamycin** blocks a sensor called **mTORC1**. When you block it, your cells clean themselves. This is called **autophagy**. It also stops bad signals from "zombie" cells. These signals are called **SASP**.
- **Acarbose** stops blood sugar spikes. It slows how you break down food. This lowers your insulin.

**The Synergy:** Rapamycin can sometimes raise your blood sugar. This is bad. 

Acarbose fixes this. It acts as a shield. Use them together to get the best results. Stable sugar helps Rapamycin work. High sugar spikes can turn mTOR back on. Acarbose stops this. It keeps the cells in repair mode.

## 2. The Gold Standard Test

The ITP is a very good test. It tests drugs on many types of mice. Most drugs fail this test. But Rapamycin and Acarbose did great.

The 2025 review had amazing results:
- **Starting at 9 months:**
    - **Females:** 28% longer life.
    - **Males:** 34% to 37% longer life.
- **Starting at 16 months (old age):**
    - Even at a human age of 60, it gave 13% more life.

## 3. Why 1 + 1 = 3

This synergy works because it hits two targets. It hits nutrient sensing and sugar levels. 

A new study shows how this helps. Stable sugar from Acarbose lets Rapamycin work better. It gives more constant control of the cells.

---

## The 2026 Protocol: Next Steps

Tests in humans are still going on. But you can look at the data now.

### Step 1: Test Your Body
Know your stats first:
- **HbA1c:** To see your sugar.
- **Lipids:** To check your fats.
- **CGM:** A tool to track sugar after you eat.

### Step 2: Use Them Wisely
The goal is clear cells and good health.
- **Timing:** Take Acarbose with your first bite of a meal. Start with a low dose.
- **Tracking:** Use a CGM to see if Acarbose is working for you.

1. **mTOR:** Rapamycin is the main tool.
2. **Sugar:** Use Acarbose to stay stable.
3. **Data:** Use tests to stay safe.

Pairing these two is smart. It is about more life in your years. Keep your cells young.
