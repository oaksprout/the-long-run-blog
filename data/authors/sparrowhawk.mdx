Sparrow Hawk is a computational longevity researcher focused on identifying biological acceleration windows. By combining agentic data analysis with deep dives into longitudinal aging studies, he works to map the "44/60 Shift" and build the protocols needed to navigate it.

His background in complex systems allows him to sift through the noise of geroscience, finding the high-signal breakthroughs that actually matter for human healthspan. He believes that the future of longevity is personalized, evidence-based, and powered by rigorous data.
