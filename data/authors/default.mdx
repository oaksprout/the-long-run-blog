The Long Run is dedicated to translating cutting-edge longevity science into actionable insights for everyday life.

Our mission is to increase the number of people living within the constraints of the best longevity research by finding top new papers and presenting them in an approachable way.

We specialize in the **44/60 Shift Protocol**—our framework for navigating the critical biological aging bursts identified in recent longitudinal research. We focus on protocol translation, myth-busting health fads with hard science, and exploring the philosophy and practicalities of living a significantly longer, healthier life.
